# Food places

The category page for cafes, restaurants, hawker stalls, and bakeries worth remembering.
Each place gets its own entity page under `wiki/entities/`; this page is the hub that groups
them by area and by what they are good for.

Convention for this category:

- One entity page per venue, even for a small stall. Aliases carry the shortened names people
  actually say.
- Every venue page records **where** it is (nearest MRT or street), **what it is good for**,
  and whether the claim is **first-hand or from a listicle**. Unvisited places stay
  `status: draft` — the vault should never imply a recommendation it cannot back.
- Malls and neighbourhoods are entities too, so venues in the same place link to a shared hub
  (e.g. [[the-woodleigh-mall]]).

## Known pitfall

Singapore cafe listicles group venues loosely by "near <MRT>" and routinely include places
that are nowhere near it — Tiong Hoe Specialty Coffee (Bukit Merah) and Wheeler's Yard
(Balestier) both show up on Woodleigh lists. Check the actual address before trusting a
list's geography (`raw/web/2026-08-15-084400-woodleigh-cafes-search.md`).
