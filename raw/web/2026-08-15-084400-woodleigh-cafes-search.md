## Cafes at / near The Woodleigh Mall (11 Bidadari Park Drive, ~5-7 min walk from Woodleigh MRT)

- **OLLA Coffee** — #01-55 The Woodleigh Mall. Helmed by the 2022 Singapore Barista
  Champion. Signature Spanish latte; also pasta, avocado toast, rice bowls, pastries.
- **Surrey Hills Grocer (Woodleigh)** — grocer + dining, Australian-inspired. Brunch plates,
  gourmet coffee, truffle fries, grilled cheese toasties.
- **Study Cafe @ Woodleigh** — study/work spot rather than a food destination.

## Slightly further (Potong Pasir, one MRT stop)

- **Rise Bakehouse** — low-key cafe known for cakes and pastries.

## Caveat

Several "near Woodleigh" listicles also name Tiong Hoe Specialty Coffee and Wheeler's Yard.
Those are in Bukit Merah and Balestier respectively — not walkable from Woodleigh.
